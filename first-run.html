ClipDuel · new-user guide · proposed streamlined app

Turn footage into clips, or text into videos. Then post.

ClipDuel has two ways to start and one way to finish. Make clips from a long video, or make videos from a script or idea. Either way, you pick what you like, then post it now or schedule one a day. This guide walks through the clip path.

STEP 1 OF 6

Drop in a long video

Podcasts, interviews, talks, and streams work best. The app needs spoken audio, because it finds moments by reading what was said.

  • Choose how many clips you want, from 1 to 5.
  • Your saved caption style is shown next to the button. Click it to change.
  • Uploading the same file again costs nothing. The transcript is cached.

Cost is about $0.006 per minute of audio, so a 45-minute episode is roughly 27¢.

STEP 2 OF 6 · first run only

Pick the captions you like, three taps

On your first run, the top moment is rendered in all four caption styles. You compare them two at a time, without names, and pick the one that reads better. The winner becomes your default.

  • Four styles, three picks, done in under a minute.
  • Both sides show the same words, so you judge only the look.
  • Names appear only after the final pick.

This is the only vote in clip mode. You can rerun it anytime from Caption style in the top bar. Video mode works differently: you pick a favorite from each job.

STEP 3 OF 6

Let it find the moments

A job runs in four stages. You can leave the page. The job keeps running and picks up where it stopped if the app restarts.

  • Transcribe: words with exact timestamps.
  • Find moments: scores each stretch on hook, flow, value, and trend, then keeps the best ones.
  • Render: trims, reframes to 9:16, and burns in captions.

A 45-minute video usually takes a few minutes. Most of that is transcription, which is skipped entirely on a re-upload.

STEP 4 OF 6

Watch what it picked

Each clip shows why it was chosen: its title, its score out of 100, and where it sits in the original video. Play any clip right on the card.

  • Post publishes now to Instagram Reels, TikTok, or both.
  • Schedule adds the clip to your one-a-day queue.
  • ⋯ → Other style re-renders just that clip in a different caption style, for when one moment needs a different look.
STEP 5 OF 6

Post it, or queue it

Write one caption, pick your platforms, and choose whether it goes out now or joins the daily queue. The same form does both, so there's nothing new to learn.

  • Only platforms you've connected can be selected.
  • A post is sent exactly once. If something fails, you retry it yourself, so the same clip never goes out twice.

Posting needs your Instagram and TikTok tokens plus a public URL set in the app's config. Until then, the Post button explains what's missing.

STEP 6 OF 6

Let the queue post one a day

Turn the daily queue on, pick a time, and it posts the oldest clip in the queue once a day. Posted clips leave the queue. If the queue is empty, nothing posts.

  • First in, first out. Remove any clip before it goes out.
  • Change the time at any point. The next post follows the new time.
What a new user won't see

Three things folded away

The old app had four tabs and three creation modes. It now has three tabs and two modes. The rest is removed, folded in, or hidden behind a flag, and your existing clips and votes are kept either way.

Folded into quick picks

Vote tab

Voting now happens where you work: the three-pick caption setup for clips, and a blind “pick your favorite” on each video job. There's no longer an endless stream of pairs to judge.

Removed

Results & leaderboards

Your picks become your defaults: a caption style for clips, and your last favorite look for videos. No ranking table needed.

Labs · off by default

Video → animate

AI-animated segments stay behind a flag until the pending quality checks pass.
